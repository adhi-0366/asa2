The challenge

Build for the campus we share.

CampusBuild is a student-led internal hackathon. Form a team, choose a real problem, and turn your idea into a working prototype with help from mentors.

What you’ll do

Over one focused 24-hour build, teams will define a problem, develop a prototype, and pitch it to a panel of faculty and industry mentors. All departments and experience levels are welcome; beginners can join a team and learn as they go.
